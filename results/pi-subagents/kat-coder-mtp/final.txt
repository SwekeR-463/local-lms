## Key Design Decisions

- **CLI**: `--input`, `--output`, `--clip_duration`, `--clip_stride`, `--fps`, `--spatial_size`, `--workers`, `--overwrite`, `--log_level`
- **Clip planning**: Deterministic `(start, end)` pairs across the full video duration, advancing by `clip_stride`
- **Tensor layout**: `C x T x H x W` with ImageNet normalization and `F.resize` to `spatial_size × spatial_size`
- **Atomic writes**: `tempfile.mkstemp` + `os.replace` to prevent partial `.pt` files
- **Resume**: Existing clips preserved by default; `--overwrite` regenerates all
- **Error handling**: Short videos, unreadable files, and corrupt inputs are logged and continued — never abort the run
- **Streaming**: Videos processed one at a time, not loaded into memory
- **pytorchvideo**: `EncodedVideo` used with graceful fallback on import failure
